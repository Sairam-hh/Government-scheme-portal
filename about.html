About Us

Simplifying Access To Public Benefits

We translate scattered government notifications into clear, comparable and trustworthy citizen information.

Our Mission

Every Citizen Deserves To Know What They Are Entitled To

Millions of eligible households never claim benefits simply because information is fragmented across departments, languages and formats. Our mission is to remove that friction with a single, calm and accurate reference layer.

Our Vision — a country where checking eligibility for a public scheme takes two minutes, not two months, and where no citizen depends on an intermediary to understand their rights.
